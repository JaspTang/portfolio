About me

  My name is Jasper. I am 17 years old and live near Phoenix, Arizona. One word I would describe myself is: curious. Whether it be through YouTube Videos or passion projects, I explore my curiosity to learn more about the world; some of my friends amiably call me a "yapper" due to the random fun facts I'll spit out. Other Interests
Canva is my best friend when it comes to Graphic Design. Over three years, I have used Canva to express my interests, from event flyers to seminar presentations. I use my designs to help many people from peers to my community. To me, engaging in graphic design is merging the values of public announcements with artistic flare. A couple of my most favorite uses of graphic design include my AP Seminar presentation on internet disinformation and a school-project on applied math in flight routing.
Sandbox games are my go-to guilty pleasure. When I have free-time, you'll find me at my computer playing games like Minecraft, Scrap Mechanic, Kerbal Space Program, and Cities Skylines. The unlimited freedom these games grant motivate me to explore my imagination and turn ideas into full-fledged projects. In these games, particularly Scrap Mechanic, I like to contribute to the community by sharing my projects. Additionally, I like to watch YouTubers create their own communities in these games to which I can contribute!
